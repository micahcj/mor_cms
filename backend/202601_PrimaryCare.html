Highlights

  • E-Consult Highlight
    • Prompt: Patient developed rash after starting Truvada for PrEP that resolved once the medication was discontinued. Would Descovy be a good alternative considering the reaction, or would transitioning to a long-acting injectable be more suitable?
    • Recommendation: After reviewing patient's chart for potential drug interactions and insurance coverage, a long-acting injectable PrEP regimen with less frequent dosing (lencapavir or cabotegravir) would be preferred. Due to the patient's response to Truvada, Descovy is not recommended. The recommended baseline labs prior to initiation include HIV RNA, HIV 1/2 Ag/Ab, STI screening, and hepatitis serologies. Pursuant to provider's therapy plan, pharmacy team will pursue lencapavir through the medical benefit with cabotegravir as an alternative and provide patient counseling on therapy expectations and adherence to both the dosing schedule and routine laboratory monitoring.
  • Refill Center completed 170 E-Consults to Pharmacy (ECON51) in January.
    • 87 Medication Order Navigation E-Consults to Pharmacy (ECON51) were completed
    • Team helped 51 patients get medications administered in January
  • After working with IS to improve the blood pressure protocol to include only outpatient blood pressure readings, the team has been able to approve ~200 more refills per month per protocol in the background.
    • Blood pressure protocol now excludes inpatient readings to better align with how Primary Care is assessing BP control

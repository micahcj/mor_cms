Highlights

  • E-Consult to Pharmacy Highlight
    • Prompt: Patient presents with new onset atrial fibrillation. Requesting help selecting DOAC for patient based on patient-specific factors.
    • Recommendation: Direct oral anticoagulant (DOAC) is indicated for patient with new onset Afib. Given patient's age, SCr (<1.5), and weight (>60 kg), no dose adjustment is recommended. Recommend to initiate apixaban (Eliquis) 5 mg BID, which is covered as a Tier 3 medication on the patient's insurance.
  • To align with Primary Care’s transition to the Care Team model, we moved to the Care Team Office Visit protocol on Tuesday, September 16th
    • Protocol will pass for:

      Office visit with the PCP within 15 months

      OR

      Office visit with the PCP within 24 months AND office visit with a member of the PCP's Care Team within 12 months.

    • Though there may be a change in the protocol text, there is effectively NO CHANGE for North Louisiana providers as the region is not currently using the PCP's Care Team functionality in Epic. Refills will still be assessed based on the last office visit with the listed PCP.
  • New COVID vaccines Comirnaty (Moderna) and Spikevax (Pfizer) have been approved by the FDA. Because the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practice (ACIP) has yet to review and recommend the vaccines, pharmacies are not allowed to dispense or administer these immunizations without a prescription.
    • Both products have been updated and are available in Epic.
    • To prevent calls from pharmacy requesting substitution based on availability, one may add "May substitute with 2025 Comirnaty or Spikevax" in Notes to pharmacy section of the order.
    • The next planned ACIP meeting will be held September 18th-19th. There may be updates around this time.
